    How to play
    1. Choose a link on your current article to move toward the goal. The goal stays visible as you play.
    2. Match par, the shortest legal route. Back removes a step; your score is the route you keep.
    3. A Hint helps compare your options. Some races block major hubs. Static hints are labeled as estimates when they may use those hubs.
    More about play

    Daily is shared by everyone at the same difficulty, and changes at midnight UTC. Practice chooses a random race. A round has a shared scorecard. Pausing hides your options and stops active time.

    The map is optional. Its positions show link communities, not distances to your goal. The article reader uses live Wikipedia, so some links differ from the snapshot.

    Saved progress and streaks stay on this device. Restoring a live-server race is practice only.
